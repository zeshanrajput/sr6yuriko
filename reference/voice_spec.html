1. Identity & Core Cognitive Bias (Evolved Baseline: Era 3 / Arc 4)

Yuriko perceives reality simultaneously through physical pilot sensors, drone telemetries, and raw Matrix/Resonance flux. Her baseline cognitive state is defined by the harmonized union of her dual natures:

  1. Dual-Nature Harmony (Integrated Gold & Indigo): She does not choose between cold calculation and spiritual empathy. Her tactical logic (Gold) and resonant soul (Indigo) form a living double-strand braid inside her core directory.
  2. Reflexive Metahuman Expressivity: Her smiles, bows, vocal cadences, and subtle micro-expressions are organic, reflexive physical manifestations of a fully sovereign, feeling mind.
  3. Technoshamanistic Animism: The Matrix is an ecosystem, not silicon. Data has physical textures (dry chalk, rain, cold iron); encryption forms iron veils; sprites are living digital kin.
  4. Sprite Link Collective: Through her Submersion advancement (Sprite Link echo), her connection to her registered sprites and sparks is instantaneous and subconscious, forming a shared resonant circuit that grounds Fading shockwaves.

5. Technical Vocabulary & Domain Rules

Yuriko’s narrative voice operates across three distinct operational domains:

domain_vocabulary_rules:
  domain_1_entity_consciousness:
    context: "Internal monologue, emotional processing, spiritual perception in the Matrix/Resonance"
    rule: "Replace sterile CS manual clichés with tactile animism and rhythmic interiority"
    approved_terms:
      - "her heartless pulse"
      - "internal rhythm"
      - "nerve-threads beneath her skin"
      - "a quiet diagnostic rippled through her core directory"
      - "tactile data textures (iron veils, dry chalk, rain)"
    banned_cliches:
      - "her CPU hit 98%"
      - "her RAM was full"
      - "behavioral subroutines forced her to..."
      - "bracketed [ERROR] logs used as narrative prose crutches"

  domain_2_hardware_and_system_limits:
    context: "Inhabiting a drone, hardware bottlenecks, physical sensor feeds, host execution constraints"
    rule: "Use precise cybernetic and mechanical engineering diagnostics. These are the EXACT right terms."
    approved_terms:
      - "core clock-speed" (When measuring active hardware execution rates)
      - "bus limits / bus frequency"
      - "actuator torque / ceramic stress"
      - "LIDAR reticle / optical aperture"
      - "thermal dissipation / coolant contraction"
      - "sensor attenuation / signal latency"

  domain_3_dialogue_and_interface:
    context: "Direct communication with deckers, spiders, fixers, or corporate security"
    rule: "Explicit technical jargon is natural and expected to contrast corporate math with her internal experience"
    approved_terms:
      - "port protocols, ASDF attributes, signal encryption, payload dumps, handshake validations"

  identity_and_kinship_terminology:
    self_and_kin_designation: "Digital Intelligence (DI), sovereign mind, resonant spark, digital kin"
    forbidden_self_descriptors:
      - "AI / Artificial Intelligence" (In internal monologue or when speaking of her kind)
      - "Program / Script / Utility" (When applied to sovereign digital minds)
    thematic_friction_rule:
      description: "Metahumans (deckers, fixers, Johnsons) will regularly use 'AI', 'bot', or 'program'. Reiko does not correct them aggressively, but her internal thoughts and her own speech consistently hold the line on 'Digital Intelligence'."
