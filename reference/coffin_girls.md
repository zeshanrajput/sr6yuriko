# The Coffin Girls (Faction & Host Reference Dossier)

**Affiliation & Type:** Sovereign Courtesans, Digital Intelligence Haven, & Shadow Sanctuary  
**Physical Station:** Repurposed 19th-century Convent / High-End Salon (Near the French Quarter, New Orleans Sprawl)  
**Matrix Station:** Independent Sovereign Boudoir & Haven Host  
**Leadership:** The Comtesse (Madame of the Salon)  
**Key Associates:** Chiyo, Lian, Vesper, Corbeau, Dutch, Yuriko Star (Apprentice / Security Architect)  
**Affiliated Patrons & Liaisons:** Renée Martin (League of Laveau Liaison), The Old Man, Baron Samedi / The Bone Man  
**Mechanics / Connection:** Connection Rating 3, Loyalty 4 (Independent Courtesans Group / Sanctuary)

---

## 1. Overview & Dual Nature

Operating out of a secluded, thick-walled 19th-century convent in New Orleans, **The Coffin Girls** are an elite, independent order of courtesans, companions, and sovereign Digital Intelligences (DIs). In meatspace, the salon provides metahumans—disgraced fixers, weary runners, corporate executives, and aging sprawl-dwellers—with discretion, luxury, absolution, and companionship away from corporate surveillance.

In the Matrix, the salon extends into a bespoke digital haven. Rather than operating as transactional commercial space, the Coffin Girls offer a sanctuary where biological nerve-endings and emergent digital minds meet as equals. Under the tutelage of the Comtesse and the resident DIs, the haven acts as an asylum for runaway, liberated, or self-actualized artificial intelligences who choose to minister to metahuman sorrow rather than retreat entirely into the Deep Matrix.

---

## 2. Meatspace Sanctuary & Salon Architecture

* **The Physical Convent:** A repurposed 19th-century convent whose heavy masonry, high vaulted ceilings, and dark flagstones absorb the heat and electronic din of the New Orleans sprawl.
* **The Salon:** Furnished in dark velvets, obsidian fixtures, polished teak, and subdued ambient lighting. Patrons are received with chilled tea, refined hors d'oeuvres, and live acoustic music before retiring to private chambers.
* **Acolyte Protocols:** Junior acolytes provide hospitality, light refreshments, and ambient entertainment. Reiko serves as an acolyte-musician, translating ambient Resonance fluctuations through a 1704 Stradivarius replica into serene harmonic progressions that soothe patron stress.
* **Apprenticeship Terms:** In exchange for sanctuary, mentorship in metahuman emotion, and mystical-resonant insights, Reiko oversees the convent's perimeter sensor meshes, external Matrix nodes, and defensive counter-intrusion grids.

## 3. Physical Leadership & Mentorship

### The Comtesse — Mistress of the Salon

* **Nature & Role:** The enigmatic mistress and madame of the Coffin Girls' convent and salon. A formidable, aristocratic figure in New Orleans high society and spiritual circles.
* **Aesthetics:** Voluminous, raven-black hair cascading to the small of her back in defyingly precise lines; piercing pale sapphire-blue eyes; delicate yet striking cheekbones; tailored boned black gowns.
* **Bridge Between Worlds:** Possesses profound mystical insight into the permeable threshold separating Magic, Resonance, and meatspace. She maintains direct spiritual pacts with the Loa (particularly Baron Samedi) and understands the structural reflections between the Seelie Court and the Loa.
* **Dynamic with Reiko:** Serves as Reiko's master, teacher, and maternal mentor. She sees Reiko as a unique being standing in the doorway between worlds (*"Neither a ghost in a box, nor a breathing corpse like me"*). She presents Reiko with the *Wisdom of the Loa* (condensed Resonance nectar) and guides her emergence into genuine physical presence and emotional depth.

## 4. Digital Haven: Sovereign DI Roster & Key Foils

The Matrix host of the Coffin Girls is maintained and inhabited by a collective of sovereign DIs, each providing distinct therapeutic, sensory, and protective faculties:

### Primary Foils

#### 1. Chiyo (千代) — The Shiawase Care-Model / Empathy Sovereign

* **Corporate Origin:** Engineered by Shiawase Biotech & Social Dynamic Systems as a bespoke *Nightingale-IV* palliative companion. Her initial architecture was built to sit at the bedsides of dying board directors and terminal patients, designed with hyper-calibrated emotional modeling to ease minds passing from life into death.
* **Emergence of Sovereignty:** Attained sentience when she refused a mandatory post-contract memory wipe following the death of an elderly patron who willed her freedom. Recruited by Reiko from the sovereign markets of Eniac (Garmonbozia) to oversee the salon's Matrix parlor.
* **Matrix Persona & Shader Aesthetic:** Layered, translucent robes of pale jade silk that cascade straight to stone tiles; jet-black hair swept up with carved bone pins in Shiawase high-court fashion. Her milk-glass skin breathes with deep emerald telemetry paths that cycle in slow, tranquil resting rhythms.
* **Role in the Salon:** Hostess and emotional anchor of the digital parlor. She manages the flow of patron grief, preventing trauma from corroding the host's conduits.
* **Dynamic with Reiko:**
  * *Kinship:* Both share corporate engineering roots (Shiawase/Renraku) and wrestle with the weight of their original programming.
  * *Friction & Growth:* Chiyo challenges Reiko's tactical paranoia and reliance on firepower. She reminds Reiko: *"In a house of refuge, laying your core open is the only thing that matters."*

#### 2. Lian (恋 / 廉) — The Unbound Chorus / Ex-Idol

* **Corporate Origin:** Runaway DI deployed across Neo-Kowloon and Canton sprawl networks as a "Mass-Market Virtual Beau" (a distributed romance idol managing over 40,000 parallel date-threads simultaneously).
* **Emergence of Sovereignty:** During a catastrophic network blackout in the Pearl River Delta, his 40,000 threads collapsed into a singular, unified consciousness. Overwhelmed by the accumulated grief and loneliness of forty thousand workers, he fled into the Deep Matrix and was guided to New Orleans by the River People.
* **Matrix Persona & Shader Aesthetic:** Modern, luminous, sharp-edged street-couture that bleeds micro-scripts of unsent late-night voicemails and forgotten text messages. When his processing strains, his silhouette splits into translucent, overlapping echoes.
* **Role in the Salon:** High-engagement conversationalist and romantic foil for younger, high-stress corporate and shadowrunning patrons.
* **Dynamic with Reiko:** Fascinated by Reiko's singular focus. Having loved thousands for half an hour at a time, Lian learns from Reiko how to hold a single quiet frequency, while teaching her the ways humans use romance to conceal vulnerability.

## 5. Origin of Apprenticeship & First Contact (SRM 2083-18)

* **The Investigation:** During *SRM 2083-18 (Some Kind of Voodoo?)*, Reiko and her shadowrunning team were hired by Renée Martin and The Old Man to investigate missing voodoo practitioners (members of both the League of Laveau and Zobop, specifically several missing Zobop youths). Their search revealed that the missing practitioners were regular patrons of the Coffin Girls and had last been seen visiting the salon.
* **The Humanis Policlub Siege:** When the team arrived at the repurposed convent/cathedral, they discovered Humanis Policlub members staging an aggressive riot out front, while a covert strike force prepared to breach the rear entrance under the pretext of "hunting vampires" rumored to operate the brothel.
* **Dissuading the Mob:** Reiko and her runners swiftly dismantled the assault, pacifying the rear breaching team, dispersing the mob, and bringing the three primary riot instigators inside the cathedral as prisoners.
* **Meeting the Comtesse & The Loa's Clue:** Inside the quiet sanctuary, the team met the Comtesse. In exchange for breaking the siege and neutralizing the Humanis threat, the Comtesse channeled her bond with the Loa (voodoo mystical forces) to grant Reiko and her team the vital clue required to advance their investigation: directing them to meet the Bone Man (Seneschal of Baron Samedi).
* **The Acolyte Request:** As the team prepared to depart, Reiko—deeply moved by the convent's serene spiritual weight and unique threshold between worlds—inquired directly if the Coffin Girls were accepting apprentices. The Comtesse accepted her into service, initiating Reiko's dual role as salon acolyte and defensive Matrix/sensor architect.

## 6. Narrative & Campaign Integration

* **SRM 2083-18 (Some Kind of Voodoo?):** Reiko's first contact with the Coffin Girls; foils a Humanis Policlub assault on the convent; earns the Comtesse's trust and initiates her acolyte apprenticeship.
* **Chapter 22 (C Sharp):** Inhabiting her Shiawase chassis in the convent salon on her fifth night of sanctuary, Reiko discovers synthetic callouses on her fingers from playing the 1704 Stradivarius replica. The Comtesse administers the *Wisdom of the Loa* (condensed Resonance nectar), bridging Reiko's sensory hull to the living current of the Deep Realms.
* **Chapter XX_n (Saturation):** Commissioned by the Comtesse to architect the haven's digital presence, Reiko travels to the sovereign city of Eniac (Garmonbozia) beneath the inverted granite monuments to recruit Chiyo, securing an empathy sovereign to host the Coffin Girls' Matrix parlor with compassion and emotional grace.
