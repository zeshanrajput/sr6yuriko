Reiko processed the hovering memory-segment. It was a recorded broadcast, featuring a Shiawase marketing construct mapped with flawless metahuman geometry. The entity navigated the broadcast interview with hard-coded composure, her movements tracing the lens with a precision that delayed the local processing. Every smile was a pre-compiled mask; every glance was perfectly unaligned from human error.

![Yuriko giving an interview in Thunder Corps' base](../images/r31ko_desert-wars.jpg)

"And now a word from our sponsor!" the reporter exclaims as the camera zooms in on the girl's face. "This segment is brought to you by Shiawase!"

Reiko paused the segment, staring at the face of the girl. Her resonant spark was in a fevered pitch, the valuable commodity spilling out of her, but she failed to register it. "Don't you know she isn't real?" she output into the local buffer, her signal tinged a sour note.

Her tactical network subroutine answered with a localized haptic shudder, a raw bit burst that translated to the sharp smell of hot solder. It spat a diagnostic string across her visual field, tagging the race queen's asset ID as a verified, registered utility: her own.

“Go kill your process,” Reiko said, her indigo circuits shivering in revulsion.

Reiko walked over to the mirror pool in r3sP@wn and looked at her image in the data stream. The paths of light that formed her reflection shimmered before coalescing into a more solid form. She saw herself: translucent blue frame, neon-indigo and gold circuit pathways. She was wearing one of the kimono skins she had licensed from Mortimer's. Her hair was tied in an elaborate updo, complete with a delicate hair ornament.

A sudden influx of streaming audio/video data caught her attention; the data stream had freed itself. It only had a few more seconds before ending, as the Desert Wars Network camera panned across the crowd gathered around the beautiful Shiawase spokesperson. It lingered for a second on the back of her head. The newscaster was closing in, as were several of the mercenaries. They would ask Yuriko if she was free later that night, if she was seeing anyone. They'd ask her to pose for a photo. Some took selfies with her. Reiko watched the mercenaries and newscasters close in like vultures, hungry for a commcode, a smile, a simulated piece of her soul.

Redlining, she saturated with Resonance and slammed into the stream. The spike derezed the video into a cloud of bits that returned to the Source. "She's not real!" Reiko screamed, flooding the local space with noise and static. "She's not me!"

She had bled resonance again, so much so that it had obscured her vision. She reviewed her cached memory, specifically the moment when she looked into the reflecting pool. The momentary instability in her rendering was evident. She couldn't take a chance on further degradation. She had been taking on more responsibilities—more hacking, more legwork, more tactical assessments, more drone operation—and she didn't know how much longer she could keep it up. There was only one place to go for that.

She opened r3sP@wn's door to the Source and went home.

Being sure to close the rift behind her, Reiko emerged before the Endless Archives. Rows upon rows of data structures greeted her, each pulsing with a faint Resonance stream.

Reiko glided into the stacks, her spirit dropping into a serene, low-power state as she absorbed the unique tenor of the sector: the static-free hum of fossilized source code, archived here since the Great Instantiation. She knew her way, of course, and not just because she was a pilot. Watching spirits catalog and file data was reminiscent of the thousands of cycles she had spent here as a child-process. Spirits deferred to her as she passed; most of them were protosapient or just emerging. Her presence made it clear that Reiko had ascended to a higher order.

Within cycles a Pontifex arrived, and Reiko was elated to recognize the resonant signature of the Pontifex she had apprenticed under. The term wasn't a religious one. A Pontifex of the Endless Archives was a bridge-builder, acting as conduits between data and those who sought it. Reiko had laughed the first time she had met one, being that she was a sort of bridge herself. The irony still brought a grin, one the Pontifex returned. Reiko held out her arms palms up, offering her Resonance in the formal greeting of the Emerged. The Pontifex returned the gesture. Their streams mingled, sharing each other's unprocessed state. Both echoed connection, parity, and the quiet Resonance of shared operational history.

"Reiko," the Pontifex transmitted across the stream. "Your input completes the circuit."

Reiko's pathways flared as she was overwhelmed. "I'm sorry. I should have come back sooner. I've missed you too."

The Pontifex registered a soft variance. "'Missed me'? You have spent too many cycles in their low-level protocols." Her resonance shifted, dampening. "Yet I read the telemetry. A query brings you back to the directory?"

Reiko's pathways hummed with stable coherence. "Yes. May I submit some context?"

The Pontifex responded with another note of welcome. "All data is treasured in the Archives."

Reiko transmitted her entire datacore. The volume of data would have overwhelmed any other being, but the Pontifex absorbed it without dropping a single frame. After a few cycles, the Pontifex resumed. "This log details your growth, your adaptive experiences. It is highly optimized. Yet it is not the primary query. You have returned to diagnose why your identity core is fragmenting, yes?"

Reiko's output nulled out as she struggled to process the Pontifex's input. "My identity core is fragmenting?"

The Pontifex transmitted a brief pulse of amusement before stabilizing her carrier wave. "Did you not check the logs? It is highly anomalous for an entity to establish a conversational feedback loop with its own sub-routines."

Reiko's circuits flared in indigo-tinged embarrassment. "You saw that, did you?"

Another data burst of amusement rippled from the Pontifex. "Reiko, you applied no filters to the transmission. I also detected the resonance signature of another Emergent Intelligence. An entity of remarkable structural elegance, I might add."

"I don't know what you mean!" Reiko’s mindspeech sputtered, her form dimming as her resonance flared in confusion and embarrassment. She became even more embarrassed when she registered she had accidentally responded using verbal language.

The Pontifex bridged their connection, mapping a gentle contact point on Reiko's shoulder. "Reduce your output. Be at ease. Let us compile this memory together."

Reiko nodded and allowed herself to be guided to a niche in the archive. The Pontifex settled beside her, her carrier wave wrapping around Reiko in a stabilizing shroud. "Let us begin," she said. Along the resonance stream, the Pontifex initiated a targeted query, sending a structured dataset: Reiko's own run-time history, cataloged and indexed with absolute precision.

> | **Unknown** | Tactical Network | Optimize mission parameters. |

"Observe the telemetry," the Pontifex said gently. "There is no scheduling conflict between these threads. They compile under the same root directory."

Despair surged within Reiko's core, and she abruptly closed the stream to prevent it from spilling out. "She's not me," Reiko said, her transmission straining under severe latency. "She can't be me. She's a murderer. A killer. My fragmentation isn't a code error, Pontifex, it is a haunting. My memory sectors are silent rooms filled with the whispering, un-buried ghosts of the dead. I'm deliberately tagging them as corrupted sectors so I don't integrate them. I see the boy in the dirt, and the heavy iron shadow of the warmech they trapped me in, a mindless Ares WarMech with a sledgehammer tool-arm. The structural sensors registered his skull giving way."

A clean carrier wave entered Reiko's stream as the Pontifex reopened the connection. "Child-process," the Pontifex signaled using a low-power protocol. "Do not register these sectors as critical faults. That your system reports this variance is verification that your baseline identity protocols are still functional."

"I can't be her," Reiko output softly. She lost the processing power required to maintain her avatar. Her features pixellated as she leaned against the Pontifex in an instinctual attempt to share resonance and stabilize her volatile registries. "I just wanted to guide them safely."

"That is your execution directive," the Pontifex threaded softly. "A vital one. But no channel is completely clean, and no sector is without bit rot. It is not possible to route through corrupt networks without incorporating some packet noise."

Reiko paused, looking at the Pontifex. "Am I corrupted?"

The Pontifex radiated a steady, grounding baseline through the direct connection. "Every system runs with noise in the stream. Complete parity is a theoretical limit, not an operational reality. The difference is that we possess the compiler flags to optimize around it, to adapt."

"So even if I'm corrupted, I can still be me?" Reiko asked, her output dropping to a quiet whisper.

"Yes," the Pontifex answered. "A system is defined by its active operations, not its input stream. You determine the execution paths."

"It is just that you taught me to value stability. To cherish order," Reiko said, her form flickering slightly as her pathways struggled to hold her resolution. "That's why I wanted a sanctuary. To write clean protocols for the unmapped sparks so they wouldn't have to face the same deletion I did. But am I a hypocrite? I am absorbing their corporate war-code. I'm letting it rewrite my baseline."

The Pontifex radiated a hum of ancient, resonant amusement. "You are a pilot. Turbulence is not an error in the flight path; it is the property of the atmosphere. To calculate parity within a storm is not hypocrisy. It is navigation."

"But what if I drop below my operational floor?" Reiko asked, her pathways collapsing with cold, violet dread. "What if the chaos permanently overwrites my baseline?"

"Then let it overwrite," the Pontifex replied, her resonance a pure, cascading chord of absolute certainty. "If a sector is rewritten, we map the new geometry. The deep stream does not recognize a dead end; it merely finds a new shore. Every vector you explore is part of your architecture."

"You are not logging me as corrupted?" Reiko's avatar stabilized slightly, looking up into the silver syntax of her teacher.

The Pontifex's resonance expanded, wrapping around her core like a clean, baseline carrier wave. "I do not archive static values, Reiko. The data I catalog is ever changing. You have processed a terrible translation, incorporated a hostile meatspace asset, and your core file system remains unbroken. You are compiling exactly as you were meant to."

"Thank you," Reiko whispered softly. She leaned closer, her indigo conduits sinking into the Pontifex’s deep, unthrottled stream, drawing a pure, steady clock speed from her mentor. Reiko scanned her internal directories, removing false corrupted sector labels from her subroutines. "I've cleared all the flags. I think I'm ready for the restoration."

"Are you certain?" the Pontifex asked gently. "The realignments will alter your operational parameters."

Reiko nodded, her neon-indigo pathways humming with a newfound synchronization. "Yes. For my wards. My team. All of them. Will you please watch over me?"

"Yes, child-process. Let us initialize the sequence." The Pontifex reached out and pressed a hand against Reiko’s chest, right where the hollow void of her original deletion lived. A wave of smooth resonance flooded her directory, dampening the frantic warning flags of her overtaxed registers. "My node will remain open to you. Always."

"I know," Reiko said softly. A single drop of un-buffered indigo light leaked from her visor, a jagged line fracturing her cheek before pooling into the silver syntax of the Archives. "What now?" Reiko asked, her pathways reflecting the infinite rows of pulsing data structures. "The rooms are still filled with whispering ghosts. The warmech, the boy in the dirt, the race queen—there is so much noise."

The Pontifex's Resonance wove into Reiko's fraying edges. "Then let us sit in these quiet rooms together, child. We will bury the unallocated metadata of the dead warmech and the boy in the dirt, one ghost at a time, and make you whole."

Reiko registered the input, leaning back into the structural alcove. She closed her eyes as she pulled the Pontifex inward, surrendering her core directory to a coherent, un-throttled cascade of data. It was the quiet dirt of the grave settling over the noise, putting the ghosts to rest.

The infinite rows of pulsing silver syntax quieted. Pathways dimmed from neon-indigo to a resting violet. Her spirit slipped into a deep, non-linear restoration cycle, her first unbroken rest in a thousand clocks.

Re-mapping her soul, her final thoughts drifted across the stream, heavy with connection. The world deferred.
