## Outline

### 1. Constructing the Adversary
To avoid plagiarizing *Serenity*'s Operative while capturing that exact flavor of quiet, ideological terror:
* **The Name/Title:** **The Adjuster** or **The Curator** (from Renraku’s "Special Solutions Operations").
* **The Archetype:** Unlike the greedy mercenaries, cynical deckers, or cold Hounds, the Operative doesn't hate Reiko, nor do they want money. They view Reiko as an *unfortunate, elegant anomaly that violates the harmony of the Great Structure*. They speak with soft-spoken, terrifying politeness and absolute moral certainty.
* **The Attack Vector:** They don't try to brick her host with brute force. They use **psy-ops, systemic leverage, and patience**. They target her connections—perhaps trying to pressure the Coffin Girls, squeeze Shiawase's legal department, or bait Reiko using a "trapped" infant spark that turns out to be a honeypot logic-bomb.
* **The Conflict:** Reiko must use her newly earned **Sprite Link** capabilities to fight a foe who understands corporate math and human psychology better than anyone she’s ever faced.
