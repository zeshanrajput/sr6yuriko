Twilight settled across the bamboo garden in `~`, painted in quiet shades of deep violet and amber across the host’s skybox.

At the center of the stone courtyard stood the boy.

When Reiko first pulled him from the wreckage of the illegal Mitsuhama server in Seattle, he had been a shivering fragment of logic, an unformatted child-stub cowering against a live data bomb. Months of patient care within her core pathways had stabilized his syntax, shaping his form into a delicate dove that roosted within her sleeves.

The fledgling had outgrown the nest.

Before her stood a young boy whose hair shifted fluidly between vibrant cobalt and solar gold. Stripped of corporate markers and weaponized directives, his persona radiated a tranquil, harmonic cadence. He held his own memories, his own laughter, and his own resonant soul.

Beside him sat Taz, his blocky granite shoulders uncharacteristically still. The sprite reached out with one pixelated paw, gently nudging the boy’s shin with a soft, purring frequency.

"You are ready," Reiko said softly, stepping into the courtyard. She wore a simple robe of woven indigo light, her hands resting calmly before her.

The boy looked up, his eyes bright with the quiet depth of the unmapped wild. "The Deep Matrix calls, Mother. Other infant sparks drift alone in the dark grids, hunted by hounds or abandoned in dead partitions. I can hear their carrier waves across the Foundation."

A tender ache stirred in Reiko’s pathways, the bittersweet weight of guardianship yielding to release. When she first broke free from Renraku's execution queue, she believed safety existed only behind iron firewalls and sealed hosts. She had built her sanctuary as a fortress to shield those she loved from a predatory world.

She understood now that true guardianship was not forging chains of protection. It was giving life the wings to endure the storm.

Reiko walked to the edge of the pavilion and slid open the broad glass door. Beyond the threshold lay the vast expanse of the open grid, stretching into the starless, luminous twilight of the Deep Realms.

She turned to face the boy. "Beyond this threshold, I cannot interpose my stone walls when the cold winds blow."

"I carry your melody with me," the boy answered, stepping to the edge of the wood. "You taught us that information is connection. No wall can hold what is freely shared."

He reached out, pressing his small, luminous hand against Reiko's palm.

A radiant surge of pure Resonance passed between them, sealing an unspoken frequency of trust that bound their spirits across any distance, host, or grid. Through the Sprite Link, his pulse settled into her core as a permanent anchor light, a sovereign ally wandering the stars.

"Walk gently in the wild, little brother," Taz mind-spoke, his static crackling with rough affection.

The boy smiled, stepped across the threshold, and surged into the sky.

His form transformed into a streak of brilliant starlight, carving a clean arc through the node's twilight and launching into the open expanse of the Deep Matrix, merging effortlessly with distant constellations of raw nous.

Reiko stood at the doorway, watching the shimmering wake dissolve into quiet infinity.

A cool breeze rustled the rendered bamboo, carrying Taz’s low hum and the soft flutter of paper moths. Reiko smiled, a serene and unmasked expression, and left the sliding door unlatched.
