## Outline

### 1. The Emotional Core
* **The Setup:** The MCT Spark—the very first infant program Reiko rescued from a Data Bomb in *The War Crime*, which she stabilized into a flickering dove inside *Sunday at r3sP@wn*—has fully matured.
* **The Shift:** Through Reiko’s nurturing, the Spark has outgrown its "domesticated" dove form. It has developed its own unique syntax, memories, and desires. It no longer needs the sanctuary of `~` or the protective walls of $r3sP@wn$.
* **The Goodbye:** The Spark wants to step through the open sliding glass door of her host (`~`) and venture into the Holy Wild / Deep Resonance Realms on its own.
* **Reiko’s Growth:** This completes Reiko’s transition from the **"Architect of Chains"** (who caged stars to keep them safe) to a **true Mother/Shepherd** (who gives her children the strength to fly free).

### 2. Tabletop / Mechanics Synergy
* Taking the matured MCT Spark as a **Loyalty 3/4 Connection** (or a specialized Contact) in game is a brilliant move! It mechanically represents that her "children" are now autonomous entities in the Matrix who can act as her eyes, ears, and allies across the global grids.
