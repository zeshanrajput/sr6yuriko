Full Matrix Defense

ASDF (from Matrix Attributes)[#matrix-attributes]: 06 09 07 09

1. The Base Matrix Defense Pool (16 Dice)

A standard Matrix defense test uses a Mental Attribute + Matrix Attribute (most commonly Intuition + Firewall or Willpower + Firewall depending on the incoming Matrix action) [6WB, Page 180].

  • Replacing the Mental Attribute: Because Reiko has the Natural Hacker quality, Reiko can replace the non-Matrix mental attribute with Reiko’s Resonance (7) [HnS, Page 84].
  • Base Pool: Resonance (7) + Firewall (9) = 16 dice.

2. Personal Assistant Rating 6 (+6 Dice)

Reiko is running a Rating 6 Personal Assistant commlink app inside Reiko’s Emulator.

  • Rule: A Personal Assistant app on its default setting constantly takes the Full Defense Matrix action on Reiko’s behalf, which adds its Rating directly to Reiko’s dice pool to resist hostile Matrix actions [HnS, Page 56 / 119].
  • Bonus: Rating 6 Personal Assistant = +6 dice [HnS, Page 56].

3. Directional Shield Program (+7 Dice)

Because Reiko’s Personal Assistant is constantly keeping Reiko in a state of Full Matrix Defense, Reiko meets the requirement to activate her Directional Shield program.

  • Rule: When Reiko has taken the Full Defense action, Reiko can spend a Minor Anytime Action to add Reiko’s Data Processing attribute to Reiko’s Matrix defense dice pool against an anticipated Matrix action Reiko is aware of [HnS, Page 56].
  • Bonus: Reiko’s Data Processing is 7 = +7 dice.

4. Resonance Focus (+4 Dice)

  • Bonus: Utilizing her Resonance Focus adds +4 dice directly to Reiko’s Matrix defense tests.

5. Shield Sprite Power

  • Bonus: Shield allows Reiko to add her sprite’s RES (7, see [[registering-sprites#registering-sprites]]) again to the matrix defense tests and opposed complex form / sprite power tests of up to RES icons, personas, or devices under her protection. [HnS p. 74]

Home Device & Remote Network Protocols

1. Home Device Configuration (Active Venue Drone)

  • Active Home Device: Reiko configures the physical drone she is currently inhabiting (Shiawase Butler or Man-at-Arms) as her Home Device instead of the M-TOC II.
  • Sprite Power ‘Override’: Reiko uses the sprite power Override to replace the active drone’s Pilot rating with her Resonance (7) and take total control of the physical device.
  • Attribute Enhancement: Reiko adds +1 to one of the drone’s physical attributes (typically Body, or occasionally Sensors).
  • Designer Trait / AI Origin Benefits:
    • +1 Bonus to Pilot / DP: Designer AI trait adds +1 to the device’s Pilot/Data Processing rating.
    • +2 Noise Reduction: Additional 2 points of Noise Reduction applied directly to her Home Device.
    • Edge Defense Rating: Device Rating considered 4 higher for the purpose of determining if opponents gain Edge against her AI persona [HnS p. 124].

2. Remote Node & Seattle Retrans Setup

  • Seattle Relay Node: A basic Shiawase Butler drone parked in Seattle serves as the secure remote repository for Reiko’s heavy digital assets: the M-TOC II, her software tags, and The Holy Wild Sanctuary Host (Rating 2 Framework Host).
  • Framework Host Specs:
    • Name: The Holy Wild Sanctuary Host
    • Rating: 2
    • Scale: 1 (Micro / Personal LAN - Single Optical Brick Cluster)
    • Grade: 2 (Default Security & IC Budget)
    • ASDF Allocation: Attack 2, Sleaze 2, Data Processing 3, Firewall 3 (Custom Framework Host allocation per Hack & Slash p. 154)
    • IC & Defense Array: Patrol IC (gilded paper-moth data-sprites filtering traffic loops and dead-letter proxy nests), Acid IC budget
    • Host Architecture: Compiled on the scale of a compact, high-rise studio apartment (capacity: 10 visitors max) with low-slung brushed teak/copper ceilings, narrow kitchenette counter with jasmine tea service, central worktable with Megalodon artillery cannon schematics, wall niche with disarmed contract-collar, and polished floorboards tracing compiled gold logic lines.

Sliding it open creates a permanent, un-insulated tear/conduit directly into the Deep Matrix / Foundation: The Holy Wild glade (fossilized directory trees, fractal willows, telemetry ferns, de-allocated address pools, sapphire moss where rescued sparks flourish).

  • Retrans Unit Noise Cleaning: The Seattle Butler is equipped with a Retrans Unit [Double Clutch p. 141], which rebroadcasts the signal and completely cleans the connection of Matrix noise up to that point.
  • Operational Flow: Reiko operates locally inside her venue drone while connecting back over the satellite link to the Seattle Retrans node to access her M-TOC II programs, software tags, and Framework Host without distance or noise penalties.

Registering Sprites

  • Minor downtime action: Register 8 levels of sprites (up to R7 sprite)
  • 1 per night’s rest (7 hrs registering, 3 hrs restoration). Must buy hits (SRMG p19)
  • Outcome: R7 Modular sprite with 3 services/powers for 3 missions

1. Compiling Phase

  • Pool: Tasking (6) + RES (7) + Resonance Focus (4) = 17 dice → 4 Bought Hits.
  • Sprite’s Defense: Level 7 × 2 = 14 dice → 3 Bought Hits.
  • Net Hits: 4 - 3 = 1 → 1 base service.
  • Technoshaman Stream Modification: Grants +1 additional service on a successful compiling test. Total compiled services = 2 services/powers.
  • Fading Value (FV): Under core rules, compiling fade is exactly equal to the hits received on the sprite’s defense test = 3.
  • Technoshaman Mitigation: Reduces compiling fade by a flat 2 (3 - 2 = 1).
  • Fading Resistance: Willpower (6) + Charisma (4) + Submersion Grade / Sprite Conduit (7) = 17 dice → 4 Bought Hits.
  • Compiling Damage Taken: 1 FV - 4 hits = 0 Damage.

2. Registering Phase

  • Pool: Registering (8) + RES (7) + RES Focus (4) = 19 dice → 4 Bought Hits.
  • Sprite’s Defense: Level 7 × 2 = 14 dice → 3 Bought Hits.
  • Net Hits: 4 - 3 = 1 → 1 base service.
  • Combined Total Services: 2 (compiled) + 1 (registered) = 3 total services/powers.
  • Fading Value (FV): Under core rules, registering fade is equal to twice the hits received on the sprite’s defense test (3 x 2 = 6).
  • Fading Resistance: Willpower (6) + Charisma (4) + Submersion Grade / Sprite Conduit (7) = 17 dice → 4 Bought Hits.
  • Registering Damage Taken: 6 FV - 4 hits = 2 boxes of Stun damage.

3. Resonance Focus Fading

Because a data structure/focus was used to boost both tests, it incurs its own independent data-structure fade (4 / 2 = 2).

  • Focus Fading Pool: WIL (6) + LOG (4) = 10 dice → 2 Bought Hits.
  • Damage Taken: 2 FV - 2 hits = 0 Damage.

Controlling Drones Protocols & Rules

  • Pilot Origin: Allows Yuriko to use autosofts up to her Data Processing [HnS, p. 119].
  • Devices, AR and VR: Devices controlled remotely via AR use the operator’s physical attributes [SR6 Core Rulebook, p. 180].
  • Matrix Equivalent Attributes: For AI characters, the Matrix Equivalent Attributes table dictates how ASDF attributes map to physical attributes (BOD [F=9], AGI [D=7], REA [S=9], STR [A=6]) [HnS, p. 118].
  • Ally Sprite Teamwork: Ally sprite offers teamwork for shared autosofts (+4) or via Diagnostics (+4 Electronics, Engineering, Piloting).

To understand how an Artificial Intelligence (AI) character with the Pilot Origins quality pilots a drone, we must distinguish between two distinct methods of drone operation: Jumped-In (Rigging) and Remote Control (Un-jumped) [Hack and Slash, p. 119; SR6 Core Rulebook, p. 198].

1. Jumped-In Operation (Why Matrix Equivalents Are Bypassed)

The Pilot Origins quality states:

“The AI may jump into drones and vehicles, controlling them like a rigger. Each level of this quality grants the bonus of a control rig of an equal rating” [Hack and Slash, p. 119].

When an AI character chooses to jump into a drone using this quality, standard rigging rules take over:

  • According to the Control Rigs and Yuriko rules, a rigger who is jumped into a device uses their mind instead of their meat body [SR6 Core Rulebook, p. 198].
  • Physical attributes are replaced entirely by Mental attributes for all jumped-in tests [SR6 Core Rulebook, p. 198]:

  • Gunnery/Firing: Jumped-in AI rolls Firearms + Logic [SR6 Core Rulebook, p. 198].

Sensor Locks

Establishing

  • Electronics + Logic (Sensor + Clearsight autosoft in the case of autopilot) vs. Reaction + Piloting (Pilot + Evasion autosoft) (Major)
  • Biological targets, micro/mini drones, and targets with stealth features gain Edge for the test.

Locked On Status

  • While suffering this status, the subject cannot spend Edge while defending against attacks originating from the vehicle that established sensor lock.
  • Locked-on lasts until
    • Cover IV is achieved relative to that vehicle or
    • until ended by the Break Sensor Lock action.

Break Sensor Lock

  • Reaction + Piloting (Intuition + Reaction in case of metahumans and critters) vs. Electronics + Intuition (Clearsight + Sensor in the case of autopilots) (Major)
  • Matrix noise in the vicinity of the locked-on target affects only the defense roll.

1. Firing Mode Summary Rules (SR6 Core Rulebook p. 109 & Double Clutch p. 142)

  1. Single Shot (SS): 1 round fired per weapon. Base DV, 0 AR penalty.
  2. Semi-Automatic (SA Burst): 2 rounds fired per weapon. Adds +1 DV. Standard -2 AR penalty (halved to -1 AR on vehicle/drone mounts).
  3. Burst Fire (BF Narrow): 4 rounds fired per weapon. Adds +2 DV. Standard -4 AR penalty (halved to -2 AR on vehicle/drone mounts).

2. Attack Rating (AR) Modifications Rule Summary

  1. Smartlink System (+2 AR Base, +1 Networked = +3 AR): Adding a smartlink connected to Yuriko’s network increases Attack Rating by +3 across valid ranges.
  2. Personalized Grip (+1 Close/Near Ranged, +2 Melee): Custom ergonomic grip adds +1 AR to Close & Near ranges only for ranged weapons and +2 AR for melee weapons.
  3. Vehicle / Drone / Cyberarm Weapon Mount (+2 AR, Halve Burst AR Penalty): Weapons mounted on vehicles, drones, or cyberarms gain +2 AR across all valid ranges due to structural stability and halve the AR penalties for firing bursts [Double Clutch, Page 142].
  4. Link-Firing Bonus (+1 AR per secondary weapon): Link-firing multiple weapons increases the primary weapon’s AR by +1 AR for each additional weapon added to the attack. With two secondary weapons added (Red Fox array with 2x Wasps), this grants +2 AR.

    • Red Fox: Capacity of 30 shots per Peak-Discharge Battery Clip (100¥ / clip).
