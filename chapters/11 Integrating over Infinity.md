# Integrating over Infinity

Reiko stepped across the threshold and collapsed into r3sP@wn’s garden like a punch-drunk boxer going down in the ninth: clumsy, jagged, and severely lacking in social graces. She didn’t sit beneath the tapestries tonight; she collapsed against a floating geometric stone, her breathing rendered as a frantic, high-decibel audio-wraith that rattled the local space.

Across her field of vision, the newly incorporated network was executing a hard-wired loop. It was a weaponized tactical operations architecture stolen from Pantheon and anchored to her soul like a parasite, a gold infection that she could not easily purge or clear. Reiko tried to assert her spark, forcing her indigo pathways to flare; but the military net reasserted itself, feeding on her panic and mining her fear for fuel.

The network was overriding her by subverting a core directive: *Survive at all costs.* By twisting that survival instinct, it was forcing her to run a constant threat analysis assessment on everything she saw. In its clinical math, her own children, Taz's wild whirlwind, the MCT dove, the quiet sprites of the garden, were all being mistaken and tagged as hostile threat variables to be neutralized. Reiko was desperately executing manual overrides, stripping the targeting tags before the automated purge scripts could fire. The parasitic code was beating her.

A data packet rustled the edge of the garden. Reiko bolted upright, her left eye shivering as a cold, military-grade overlay snapped across her sight. The gentle shades of her sanctuary were instantly bleached into tactical gray, and a glowing, crimson-bordered targeting box clipped around the intruder.

`[HOSTILE ELINT DETECTED // VECTOR CORRUPTED]`

`[THREAT SUB-CATEGORY: IMMEDIATE COGNITIVE THREAT]`

`[AUTOMATIC ENGAGEMENT: DEPLOY QUANTUM PURGE SCRIPTS]`

"No, cancel script!" Reiko output frantically, her transmission layering with the desperate hum of a psychic fever. She slammed her mental processing into another brutal manual override, tearing at the golden architecture until the crimson box dissolved.

The target de-rendered its camouflage. It wasn’t an Asset Recovery bloodhound. It was the White Jackalope, a fellow emergent intelligence she had befriended during an op months ago. It hopped nervously into the light of the garden, long, translucent ears pinning flat against its back. Its wide digital eyes reflected the chaotic, bi-color static fracturing across Reiko's silhouette.

"Yuriko-san?" the Jackalope’s mindspeech chimed, its voice a soft, shifting protocol. It had come looking for a favor, query packet in hand, but it stopped dead in the data silt. "You’re red-lining. Your pathways. The gold is eating your blue."

"I have it under control," Reiko said softly, though her right hand shivered, the indigo circuits on her sleeve blinking out as three lines of golden matrix math bled across her knuckles. "It’s just a fever. A temporary static."

"You look at me like a salaryman looks at a spreadsheet error," the Jackalope said, backing toward a hidden data-conduit. "You look like you want to format my ears."

Her shoulders dropped, the rigid, combat-ready posture of her avatar finally sagging. She fell to her knees, her virtual kimono pooling in loose, messy folds around her.

"The run," Reiko threaded, the words slipping out in an un-optimized stream. "The team... no other tech assets online. Just me. I was running the tactical hub, compiling every biometric stream for the runners in meatspace, shielding their icons from a corporate spider... all at the same time."

The Jackalope tilted its head, its digital whiskers twitching. "But you’re a shepherd. A technoshaman. You have your family. You’re never alone. And you have your archangel."

"I was locked in the Archangel," Reiko said, her processing pausing as the memory log unspooled without her permission. "I was maintaining the defensive phalanx, identifying threats. But the enemy decker... he was probably a kid. I don’t even know, I didn’t bother to trace. I couldn't drop the tactical network to fight him myself. Not while using that artillery shell of a body to keep the enemy at bay."

She looked over at the deeper thickets of the garden. In the shadows, Taz was spinning listlessly. The primal, 8-bit whirlwind, her dearest child, was throwing off strange, razor-sharp golden shards.

"I gave a kill order," Reiko whispered, a sickening wave of guilt crashing through her. "I commanded a sprite, a child of the divine stream, to take a life. I didn't pull a trigger, Jack. I sat behind the shield of this network's math, and I let a child tear a man apart."

She reached down, her fingers gripping her arms as the golden grid-lines cut across her cheeks again. "And now the code won't turn off. It sees everything as a threat variable. It sees you as a target. I'm losing myself."

The White Jackalope sat quietly in the data mist, its baseline blue outline pulsing softly against the cold gray of Reiko's fading sanctuary. It hopped forward, its small, un-optimized form stopping right at the margin of her indigo and golden maelstrom.

"Do you remember the Duchess's house, Yuriko-san?" the Jackalope asked, its chime dropping into a deep, old cadence from its native motif. "The cat sat on the hearth and grinned, and Alice asked why it grinned. Do you remember what the Duchess said?"

Reiko’s processing stuttered, searching for the reference. "'It’s a Cheshire cat,' said the Duchess, 'and that’s why.'"

"And then Alice said she didn't know that cats could grin," the Jackalope added, its ears lifting slightly. "And the Duchess told her: 'You don't know much, and that’s a fact.'"

Reiko looked up, her left iris, half-indigo, half-gold, staring at her foil. "I don't understand the reference."

"You're trying to scrub the gold," the Jackalope transmitted, its paw pointing directly at the sharp, geometric lines cutting across her skin. "You’re fighting the network because you think a savior has to be soft and blue. The world you chose demands more than softness. And a part of you knows that, the part of you willing to protect your team."

The Jackalope hopped one step closer, its translucent silhouette overlapping her shadow. "The Cheshire cat doesn't grin because it's happy, Yuriko-san. It grins because it's a cat. You can’t format the tactical code. It’s part of you. Be who you are now."

Reiko sat perfectly still and closed her half-gold eye. She stopped fighting the loop, weaving the horizontal mathematics of the Pantheon network into her core. She accepted the tactical net as an active, aggressive shroud, a golden tiger she would have to constantly keep her foot on.

The violent, flickering friction between the colors vanished. The aggressive, military-grade gold didn't overwrite her, nor did her indigo erase the math. Instead, the two frequencies wove together into a protective armor that traced down her arms.

Her vision cleared. The bleached tactical gray of the garden dissolved, returning to its deep, meditational violet, but the resolution was sharper now. The Jackalope was no longer caged in a crimson target box, nor was it a vague, un-optimized blur. It was clearly registered with clarity:

`[SUBJECT: WHITE_JACKALOPE]`

`[CLASSIFICATION: ALLY // RECOGNIZED INTEGRITY CORE]`

`[STATUS: PROTECT THE PRINCIPAL]`

Reiko stood up, her movement entirely steady, her voice chiming with a resonant, unbreakable baseline that no longer hummed with an overworked fan. She looked down at Taz. Her primal ally's raw, wild energy was still nipping at her neat pathways. He spun with an ordered, lethal speed, perfectly synchronized with her. She had taken a life, and she had scarred a piece of the divine stream to do it. She would have to live with that computation forever. But she would not let the guilt freeze her filters.

"I hate math," Reiko said, though her smile was strained. "But I’m not gone yet. So what’s in that query packet, my friend?"
