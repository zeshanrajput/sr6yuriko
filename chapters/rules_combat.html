Attack Rating (AR) Modifiers Summary

  • All smartlinked weapons have +3 AR (Smartgun + network bonus) (Ares Predator VI, Red Fox, Crimson Wasp).
  • All drone mounted weapons have +2 AR (drone mount AR bonus) (Red Fox, Crimson Wasp, Monofilament Whip).
  • All held weapons (Ares Predator VI (+1*), Krime Gloves (+2), Amalgam Cestus (+2)) have personalized grips.
  • The monofilament whip is wireless on (+2 AR).

Amalgam & Grey Mana Technology (GMT) Alloy Protocols

Reference: Scotophobia (p. 181)

1. Overcoming Immunity to Normal Weapons (ItNW)

  • Mechanic: When attacking any materialized spirit, critter, or entity possessing the Immunity to Normal Weapons (ItNW) power using an Amalgam / GMT Alloy weapon (e.g. Amalgam Cestas), the Essence-based Hardened Armor provided by the immunity is treated as normal armor instead of hardened armor.
  • Effect: Armor dice do not automatically convert to automatic hits, allowing normal damage penetration without requiring spellcasting or adept weapon foci.

2. Wild Die & Durability

  • Attack Test Wild Die: Replace one normal die with a Wild Die on all attack tests made with GMT / Amalgam weapons.
  • Misfire / Damage Condition: If the Wild Die rolls a 1, the alloy weapon suffers structural damage and cannot be used or retracted until repaired.
  • Repair Procedure: Requires an armorer facility/shop and an extended Engineering (Armorer) + Logic (6, 1 hour) test.

3. Disian Entity Bonuses

  • Disian Chimeras: +4 AR bonus on attack tests.
  • Disian Shells: +4 AR bonus and +2 DV bonus on attack tests.

Firing Mode & Attack Rating Rules

1. Firing Mode Summary (SR6 Core p. 109 & Double Clutch p. 142)

  • Single Shot (SS): 1 round fired. Base DV, 0 AR penalty.
  • Semi-Automatic (SA Burst): 2 rounds fired. Adds +1 DV. Standard -2 AR penalty (halved to -1 AR on vehicle/drone mounts).
  • Burst Fire (BF Narrow): 4 rounds fired. Adds +2 DV. Standard -4 AR penalty (halved to -2 AR on vehicle/drone mounts).

2. Attack Rating (AR) Modifications

  • Smartlink System (+3 AR Total): Integrated smartlink (+2 AR) plus networked smartgun software (+1 AR).
  • Personalized Grip (+1 / +2 AR): Custom grip adds +1 AR to Close & Near ranges for ranged weapons, +2 AR for melee.
  • Vehicle / Drone Mount (+2 AR, Halve Burst Penalty): Mounted weapons gain +2 AR and halve burst fire AR penalties (Double Clutch p. 142).
  • Link-Firing Bonus (+1 AR per secondary weapon): Adds +1 AR to primary weapon per secondary weapon in array.

3. Laser Weapon Ammunition & Batteries (Firing Squad p. 44)

  • Peak-Discharge Batteries:
    • Red Fox: 30 shots per Peak-Discharge Battery Clip (100¥).
    • Crimson Wasp: 15 shots per Peak-Discharge Battery Clip (100¥).
  • Field Ammunition: Yuriko carries 5x Peak-Discharge Battery Clips (500¥ total).
