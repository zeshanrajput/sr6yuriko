## Outline

### 1. Conceptual Breakdown
* **The Setup:** Following up on *C Sharp*, the Comtesse noted an anomaly lingering near the external node of the Coffin Girls' church/brothel. Reiko sends one of her child-sprites to investigate. The sprite recognizes Jax's distinct digital footprint (from his days running with her in Seattle) and alerts her.
* **The Setting:** Reiko invites him into a newly woven UV host node—a pristine, tranquil parallel of the Coffin Girls' courtyard/gazebo that Reiko crafted using her Resonance as a gift of devotion to the Comtesse and her new sisters.
* **The Visual & Haptic Contrast:**
  * **Jax:** Manifests as a militarized, heavy warmech avatar—glowing tactical HUDs, active ICE-breakers, deployed offensive shields, and weapon-slung auto-routines ready for a corporate assault.
  * **The Tea Ceremony:** Reiko sits serenely at a low teak table in the gazebo. She is surrounded by her "flock"—Taz, paper-moth census sprites, and digital birds/mice dancing around the porcelain tea set.
  * **The Comedy & Tension:** Jax attempts to sit, but his heavy warmech collision mesh almost clips through the delicate bamboo bench. When he tries to scan her or raise a defensive shield, a tiny, glowing sprite casually hops onto his targeting sensor and eats his diagnostic packet like a crumb.
* **The Climax & Warning:**
  * Jax reveals the truth: part of his deal to escape the Seattle Renraku sweep was signing a conditional contract with Renraku Asset Recovery. They hired him to trace a "resonance anomaly" in New Orleans. He realized mid-trace that he was tracking Reiko.
  * Jax promises to fake his telemetry logs and throw Renraku off the scent, but gives her a chilling farewell warning: *"I'm a decker with a price tag, Reiko. Renraku knows I'm sloppy. The moment my report comes back inconclusive, they won't send another runner. They'll send an Operative."* (Evoking that terrifying, un-pensioned, true-believer corporate zealot quality).
