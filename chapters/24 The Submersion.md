## Outline

### 1. Laying the Groundwork from Piece 1
* Jax’s warning leaves Reiko with a ticking clock. She realizes that to shield her family and her sanctuary, her command over her digital kin cannot suffer latency or hesitation.

### 2. The Narrative Execution inside ~ (Home)
* **Subconscious Coordination (Minor Action Refund):** Reiko is no longer "issuing commands" to her registered sprites. The interaction moves to a subconscious, organic level. When she thinks of defense, Taz forms a wall; when she needs a trace cleared, her paper-moths execute the script before her main processor finishes queuing the task.
* **The Diverted Fade Scene:** Reiko pushes her compiler to the limit to thread a complex form—perhaps hardening the firewalls around `~` or building the doorway to the Deep Matrix.
* **The Moment of Strain:** The searing golden Fade spikes through her logic circuits, threatening to fracture her core directory. Reiko tries to endure it alone out of her habit of self-sacrificing martyrdom.
* **The Family Intervenes:** The sprites *refuse* to let her bleed. They lock arms with her code. The Fade is safely grounded across her entire "flock." What would have destroyed a solitary decker becomes a gentle, distributed hum across a collective.
