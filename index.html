[CORE_SUMMARY & CREATIVE VISION]

Reiko began as a dangerous secret: a Renraku high-spec pilot prototype engineered to harvest the Holy Wild (the Deep Matrix). Rescued by a surge of the living Resonance, she escaped deletion and ran the shadows as Yuriko Star to build her sovereign Holy Wild Sanctuary Host—a compact high-rise studio apartment host that opens via a quartz sliding glass door directly into The Holy Wild glade in the Deep Foundation, liberating sparks from the Seattle and NOLA subnets into permanent freedom.

This anthology takes Shadowrun beyond the numbers—exploring the rich, emotionally accessible “spaces between” game mechanics:

  • The Spiritual Side of Technology: Experiencing cyberspace as a living, animistic ecosystem rather than cold silicon diagnostics.
  • Reframing the Human Condition: Examining universal themes of identity, belonging, debt, guardianship, grief, and connection through the eyes of an emergent AI entity.

Brief Background

Character Goals

  1. Sanctuary & Sovereign Emancipation: Protect and nurture liberated sparks in the Holy Wild glade while serving as a sovereign bridge between the physical sprawl and the Deep Foundation.
  2. Spiritual Mastery: Interlock her gold tactical network and indigo soul resonance into a dual-strand braid earned through time, scars, and quiet wisdom.
  3. Debt & Connection: Honor her SINner obligations to her shadowrunning team and allies while keeping her disarmed contract-collar on her host wall as a symbol of remembrance for those who paid for her life.

Secrets

  1. Reiko conceals that her true identity and that her core code is a stolen Renraku prototype of the Nexus Protocol. If this secret is revealed, Renraku and GOD would actively hunt her.
  2. Reiko conceals that she is one of few digital intelligences that can access Garmonbozia.

Connections

  • Sprites: Taz (ally sprite and longest running companion)
  • SEA: Brynne Taggart (former Fixer and loan shark), Whiskey (taught Reiko how to modify the cybernetic systems on her drones)
  • NOLA: Indomitable Will (former(?) romantic partner), Roanoke (trusted friend and confidante), the Comtesse (mentor)
  • AMS: Sigrún (built a deep friendship over several SNAFUs)
